About

Your Name is a painter and illustrator whose work moves between observational landscape and imagined interiors.

Trained at [Art School], they work mainly in oil on linen and ink on paper, building images slowly through layered glazes and fine linework. Their illustration clients include publishers, magazines and independent brands.

Artist statement

I paint the in‑between moments: the light on a kitchen table before anyone wakes, a field after rain. I'm interested in how ordinary places hold memory, and how colour can carry a feeling more directly than words.
